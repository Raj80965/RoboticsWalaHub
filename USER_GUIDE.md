## 🌟 1. System Overview (परिचय)

**Robotics Wala Hub** ek comprehensive, enterprise-grade robotics laboratory management aur research ecosystem hai. Isme do alag-alag roles hain:

1. **🎓 Student Role**: Engineering students, robotics club members, aur researchers ke liye.
2. **🛡️ Admin Role**: Lab Incharge, Professors, Mentors, aur Lab Administrators ke liye.

---

## 🎓 2. STUDENT USER GUIDE (विद्यार्थी कैसे इस्तेमाल करेंगे)

```
[Register Account] ➔ [Admin Approval] ➔ [Login & Home Dashboard] ➔ [QR Attendance / Bookings / Projects]
```

### 🔹 Step 1: Registration (खाता बनाना)
1. App open karein aur **"Register as Student"** par click karein.
2. Apna **Full Name**, **Email Address**, **Password**, **College Name**, **Branch** (e.g. Robotics & Automation), aur **Academic Year** bharein.
3. Register karne ke baad account **"Pending Approval"** screen par chala jayega jab tak Admin access approve nahi karta.

---

### 🔹 Step 2: Profile & Photo Setup (प्रोफाइल और फोटो अपलोड)
1. Login hone ke baad bottom bar me **"Profile"** tab par tap karein.
2. **Photo Upload**: Circle Avatar ke upar bane **📷 Camera Badge** par tap karein. Phone gallery se apni photo select karein. Photo instantly cloud me save hokar visible ho jayegi!
3. **Student ID**: Aapko ek unique high-tech Student ID (e.g. `RWH-8F2A19`) automatic milegi.
4. **Edit Details**: **`✏️ Edit`** button dabakar apna Name, College, Roll Number, Branch aur Year kabhi bhi update kar sakte hain.

---

### 🔹 Step 3: QR Code Lab Attendance (डिजिटल अटेंडेंस लगाना)
1. Home screen par **"📷 Scan Attendance QR"** button par tap karein.
2. Camera scanner khulega. Lab me Admin ki screen par dikh rahe dynamic QR Code ko scan karein.
3. **Clock-In**: Lab me enter hote hi check-in ho jayega.
4. **Clock-Out**: Lab se nikalte waqt dobara scan karein — system automatically aapke lab working hours calculate karke attendance percentage update kar dega!
5. Apni poori purani attendance history dekhne ke liye **"Attendance History"** card par click karein.

---

### 🔹 Step 4: Lab Slot & Workstation Booking (लैब वर्कस्टेशन बुक करना)
1. Dashboard par **"🗓️ Book Lab Slot"** par tap karein.
2. Desired **Date**, **Time Slot** (Morning, Afternoon, Evening), aur **Workstation/Equipment** (e.g. 3D Printer Bench, Soldering Station, Drone Test Bed) select karein.
3. **"Submit Booking Request"** dabayein. Request Admin ke paas approval ke liye jayegi. Status aapke dashboard par real-time dikhega.

---

### 🔹 Step 5: Robotics Project Management (प्रोजेक्ट बनाना और अपडेट करना)
1. Dashboard par **"🚀 Projects"** section me jayein.
2. **"Create New Project"** click karein: Project Title, Category (e.g., Autonomous Rover, Robotic Arm, Drone), description aur team members add karein.
3. Project progress slider se progress % update karein (0% se 100%).
4. Daily milestones aur hardware requirements log karein.

---

### 🔹 Step 6: Weekly Tasks & Milestones (टास्क और डेडलाइन्स)
1. Home screen par **"Weekly Tasks"** card me aapke ongoing tasks dikhte hain.
2. Task complete hone par checkbox tap karein — wo automatically **Completed** mark ho jayega.

---

### 🔹 Step 7: Hardware Catalog & Component Request (हार्डवेयर कंपोनेंट देखना)
1. Home screen se **"📦 Hardware Catalog"** open karein.
2. Lab me available Sensors (LiDAR, Ultrasonic, IMU), Microcontrollers (Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi), aur Motors ki live stock quantity check karein.

---

### 🔹 Step 8: Project Expenses & Budget (प्रोजेक्ट का बजट और खर्चे)
1. Home screen se **"💰 Project Budget & Expenses"** par tap karein.
2. Apne project ke hardware purchase ka bill amount, item name, aur receipt details log karein taaki lab budget se approval mil sake.

---

### 🔹 Step 9: Achievements & Leaderboard (सर्टिफिकेट और रैंकिंग)
1. **"🏆 Leaderboard"** me jakar top students ki performance ranking check karein.
2. **"Verified Achievements"** me jakar apne Robotics competitions ke certificates aur awards submit karein.

---

### 🔹 Step 10: Notices & Events (सूचना पटल और वर्कशॉप्स)
1. **📢 Notices**: Lab mentor dwara bheje gaye zaroori updates real-time me dekhein.
2. **📅 Events**: Aane wali Robotics Workshops, Hackathons aur Guest Lectures ki details dekhein.

---

### 🔹 Step 11: Continuous Sliding Achievement Bar (स्लाइडिंग अचीवमेंट बार)
1. Student Home Dashboard ke top par continuous auto-sliding achievement showcase display hota hai jo har 3.5 seconds me smoothly rotate hota hai.
2. Kisi bhi achievement card par click karke full-resolution photo, award description, event date, aur official verification badge modal me dekh sakte hain.
3. Bottom dots indicator ya left/right navigation arrows se manual swipe bhi kar sakte hain.

## 🛡️ 3. ADMIN COMMAND CENTER GUIDE (एडमिन क्या-क्या करेंगे)

```
[Admin Command Center] ➔ [Approve Students] ➔ [Generate QR Attendance] ➔ [Manage Inventory, Budget & Approvals]
```

### 🔸 Step 1: Admin Login & Overview
1. Admin credentials se login karein.
2. Top dashboard par total active students, ongoing projects, total lab hours, aur pending requests ki live metrics dikhegi.

---

### 🔸 Step 2: Student Verification & Directory (छात्रों को अप्रूव करना)
1. Bottom bar me **"Directory / Students"** tab par tap karein.
2. Jo bhi naye students register karenge, unka card **"Pending"** status me dikhayi dega with unki photo aur details.
3. **`✅ Approve Lab Access`** button dabate hi student ka account instantly active ho jayega!
4. Kisi bhi student ki profile photo, branch, email aur live attendance percentage check kar sakte hain.

---

### 🔸 Step 3: Dynamic Attendance QR Code Generator (अटेंडेंस क्यूआर कोड जनरेट करना)
1. Admin Dashboard par **"📷 Generate Attendance QR"** button par click karein.
2. Screen par lab ka active QR code display hoga.
3. Ise lab screen/projector par display karein taaki students aate-jaate scan kar sakein.

---

### 🔸 Step 4: Lab Slot Booking Approvals (वर्कस्टेशन बुकिंग अप्रूव करना)
1. **"Pending Bookings"** list me jayein.
2. Check karein ki workstation available hai ya nahi.
3. **"Approve"** ya **"Decline"** button dabayein. Student ko instant alert chala jayega.

---

### 🔸 Step 5: Hardware Inventory Management (हार्डवेयर स्टॉक मैनेज करना)
1. **"⚙️ Hardware Inventory"** tab me sabhi lab equipment ki list dekhein.
2. Naye components add karein, stock quantity update karein, aur kis student ne konsa component issue karwaya hai wo track karein.

---

### 🔸 Step 6: Project Budget & Expense Audits (खर्चे पास करना)
1. **"🛡️ Admin Budget"** me students dwara submit kiye gaye hardware purchase receipts check karein.
2. Bills verify karke **Approve** karein taaki unhe lab grant ya reimbursement mil sake.

---

### 🔸 Step 7: Broadcast Notices & Event Publishing (नोटिस और इवेंट्स डालना)
1. **Notice Publish**: Koi bhi lab announcement, rule change ya urgent alert likhkar sabhi students ko broadcast karein.
2. **Event Create**: Nayi Robotics Hackathon ya Workshop ki date, time aur venue add karein.

---

### 🔸 Step 8: Reports & Analytics (रिपोर्ट्स और डेटा एनालिसिस)
1. **"📊 Analytics"** tab me lab utilization graphs, top active projects, aur attendance trends dekhein.
2. Monthly/Annual reports generate karke download ya audit ke liye export karein.

---

### 🔸 Step 9: Achievement Banners Manager (अचीवमेंट फोटो अपलोड और मैनेज करना)
1. Admin Command Center me **"🏆 Achievement Banners Manager"** card par click karein.
2. **Nayi Photo Upload**:
   - Title (e.g. "Smart India Hackathon 1st Prize"), Winner Names, Event/Date enter karein.
   - **"📸 Choose Banner Photo"** se photo select karein (JPG/PNG).
   - **"Publish Achievement Banner"** dabate hi showcase instant sabhi student dashboards par live ho jayega!
3. **Manage & Delete**: Purani photos ko preview karein aur **"Delete Banner"** se kabhi bhi hata sakte hain.
